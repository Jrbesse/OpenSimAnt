# Batch B: ambient themes and short pieces (provenance of independent composition)

Written from docs/clean_pack/music.md alone, by `make_music_b.py` (notes are in that script as note-name strings). One line per song: what it is, key, shape / motif idea, tempo.

- **10003** (Ambient 1 swaying): ambient theme 1: a relaxed 6/8 sway; a soft four-bar tune on the tuned drum over a bell glow; a lower middle phrase, a hushed section with the tune moved to the bells, a quiet settled close on F. Key: F major. Tempo: 90 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~56.0 s).
- **10004** (Ambient 2 scurrying): ambient theme 2: a quick scurrying patter of light sixteenths on the tuned drum with a tick/shaker/soft-kick bed; A-section cells (rising triads, small turns) twice varied, a syncopated B section twice, a hushed C section, a patter fade-out onto a low A. Key: A dorian. Tempo: 160 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~48.0 s).
- **10005** (Ambient 3 skipping): ambient theme 3: brisk and curious, a skipping dotted rhythm; a bell tune with a lydian (raised fourth) twist over a pitched log-drum ostinato, soft kick, a light crack on 2 and 4 and a sparse shaker; contrasting minor-side B section, a pizzicato hushed section, a coda that ends on a held B-flat. Key: B-flat lydian. Tempo: 144 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~41.7 s).
- **11002** (Moving on): travelling flourish: four sixteenth runs, each starting a step higher over a changing chord, then a small lift (F#-A-B-D) onto a held D major. Key: D major. Tempo: 240 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~5.0 s).
- **11003** (Go): the 'go!' flourish: seven quick sixteenths climbing a pentatonic scale from D4 and a held top G5, plucked voice only. Key: D (major pentatonic with a flat seventh). Tempo: 180 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~1.3 s).
- **11004** (Ta-da): spare cheerful ta-da: a rising pick-up arpeggio and a tumbling answer to a held C on the lead, an oom-pah bass, four ticks and two tuned drum strokes. Key: C major. Tempo: 240 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~2.0 s).
- **11005** (Oh well): comic droop: two falling triad-skips (G-E-C, F-D-B: the B is the joke) then a wobble between A and A-flat before sagging onto a long G; soft chords C, F, Dm, C underneath. Key: C major. Tempo: 240 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~4.0 s).
- **11006** (Exchange): a spell: slow eighth-note arpeggios whose long notes overlap into a shimmer, rising for four bars through A lydian (the raised fourth D# gives the magic) and sinking back, settling on a calm A major chord. Key: A lydian. Tempo: 180 bpm (grid unit 120 ticks; ~10.7 s).
- **11007** (Sting): spare ominous sting: three falling pairs of notes (Bb-A, G-F#, Eb-D), each a half step or step lower, the last one dropping onto a long low D. Key: D (Bb-A, G-F#, Eb-D). Tempo: 181 bpm (grid unit 120 ticks; ~2.7 s).
- **11013** (Recruit): call to arms 'come on, everybody': three short chord stabs climbing in inversions, a D-minor lean, then a bright held C major chord; top key G5 so it survives +12. Key: C major (works at +6 and +12). Tempo: 180 bpm (grid unit 120 ticks; ~2.7 s).
- **11016** (Dismissed): 'dismissed, off you go': a lazy falling line from G5 with one small upturn (A-B-A) and a settled G4; top key G5. Key: C major. Tempo: 120 bpm (grid unit 120 ticks; ~4.0 s).
- **11018** (Alarm): alarm: a sharp two-note figure E-Bb, three times, the last one left ringing; plain unison, no harmony. Key: E / B-flat (tritone pair). Tempo: 240 bpm (grid unit 120 ticks; ~4.0 s).
- **11020** (Black queen): dignified: a rising broken Eb arpeggio (Bb-Eb-G), a stately turn and a settled Eb, under slow plain chords I, V, I. Key: E-flat major. Tempo: 96 bpm (grid unit 120 ticks; ~5.0 s).
- **11021** (Red queen): sly creeping: an eight-bar winding line of slow-stepping eighths that sidle chromatically (G#, D#) through A minor, with a low drone changing only once a bar; ends back on A. Key: A harmonic minor. Tempo: 181 bpm (grid unit 120 ticks; ~10.6 s).
- **20000** (Queen joy): burst of delight: a four-note upward spring to a high C, a quick echo (E-G-C) and a long C chord. Key: C major. Tempo: 220 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~2.2 s).
- **20001** (Nest founded): satisfied upward phrase: two rising steps up a sixth chord (A-C#-E-F#, D-E-F#-A) then G-A-B and home on A. Key: A major. Tempo: 221 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~2.2 s).
- **20002** (Reds gone): relieved and lively: bouncy syncopated lead over I, IV, V, I with a walking bass; ends with a full G close (a deliberate last bar of held G chord and G bass). Key: G major. Tempo: 120 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~8.0 s).
- **20003** (Human moves in): wry 'uh-oh': a staccato chromatic creep (C-C#-D-D#) over minor stabs, a falling two-note gasp twice, pulsing repeated notes tightening by half steps, then a held G with a G7 chord left hanging for 20004. Key: C minor, ending unresolved on G7. Tempo: 120 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~7.0 s).
- **20004** (The end): the jolly 'the end': picks up on the C that 20003 withheld; a bouncing C arpeggio, a stepwise cadence (F-E-D-B-C) over V to I; heard right after 20003 (20003 ends on G, 20004 answers in C). Key: C major. Tempo: 120 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~4.0 s).
- **20005** (Final fanfare): grand finale: a heroic E-flat theme (repeated-note motif, a leap to Bb, a sequence climbing to a high Eb) over I-IV-vi-V-I with kick and bass, and a slower final chord with bell (tempo 120 then 90 for the last eight sixteenths). Key: E-flat major. Tempo: 120 bpm, then 90 bpm from unit 56 (grid unit 60 ticks; ~8.3 s).
- **20007** (Lament one): slow drooping lament: each four-bar phrase of the pluck sags by a step or two (A-G-F-E, F-E-D, D-C-Bb-G, F-E-D) over i, VI, iv, i chords; a small ritardando at the end. Key: D minor. Tempo: 90 bpm, then 80 bpm from unit 18 (grid unit 120 ticks; ~8.2 s).
- **20008** (Lament two): tired trudge: a long-short plodding lead step by step down D-C-Bb-A and back to G, a pluck plodding on beats 1 and 3 with falling bass roots G, Eb, C, D, G; slows slightly in the last bar. Key: G minor. Tempo: 120 bpm, then 100 bpm from unit 24 (grid unit 120 ticks; ~8.4 s).
- **20009** (Lament three): trembling descent: thirty-two sixteenths in a shiver between two neighbouring semitones, each pair a half step lower, from G5 down to C5 (seven semitones); the last note is held. Key: chromatic, G5 to C5. Tempo: 128 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~3.9 s).
- **20010** (Lament four): a sigh: a long G falling stepwise (G-F#-E over Am) then D-C-B over Em with the C as the 'sigh' semitone, settling on B. Key: E minor. Tempo: 112 bpm (grid unit 120 ticks; ~4.3 s).
- **20011** (Lament five): low dirge: slow tolling F, F, C on the pluck (the C a fifth below) over one held F minor chord; four and a quarter beats at 60 bpm. Key: F minor. Tempo: 60 bpm (grid unit 60 ticks; ~4.2 s).
