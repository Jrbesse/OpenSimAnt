The remake for Windows, macOS and Linux. Download the one for your system:

- **Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)**: OpenSimAnt-...-windows.zip. Right-click it, choose **Extract All...**, then
  double-click **OpenSimAnt.exe** in the extracted folder. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC",
  click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the build isn't signed).
- **macOS 12 or newer (Apple Silicon or Intel)**: OpenSimAnt-...-macos.zip. Double-click it, then right-click
  **OpenSimAnt.app** and choose **Open** (on macOS 15 and newer: try to open it once, then System Settings >
  Privacy & Security > **Open Anyway**). The app is signed ad hoc, not by an Apple developer.
- **Linux (x86_64)**: OpenSimAnt-...-linux.tar.gz. `tar xzf` it and run `OpenSimAnt/OpenSimAnt.x86_64`.

On the first screen, choose **Open assets** to play with the remake's own art, text, sound and music: it
needs nothing else. If you own the original DOS game, **Original look** plays with your copy instead (pick
the folder with SIMANT.EXE in it). HOW_TO_PLAY.txt in each package has the details. No original game data
is included.
